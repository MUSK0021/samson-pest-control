Vegetation Control

Weeds gone. And they stay gone all season.

Tired of spraying weeds and watching them come back all season? Total vegetation control eliminates weeds and unwanted plants around your home, facility or roadway, and keeps them away the entire season. It is a bare ground treatment, handled by a technician certified in the Right of Way and Weeds category.

Services

Benefits of vegetation control.

  • Improves appearance

    Clean edges and clear ground make any property look cared for.

  • Reduces labor costs

    Less time and money spent on grounds maintenance.

  • Safer sight lines

    Clear views of signs and traffic improve vehicle safety.

  • Reduces fire hazards

    Dry, overgrown vegetation is fuel. Bare ground is not.

  • No rodent harborage

    Removes the cover mice and rats use to hide and travel.

  • Fewer allergens

    Removes allergy causing vegetation for a safer work environment.

Is vegetation control only for businesses?

No. We treat driveways, sidewalks, patios and fence lines at homes, plus parking lots, roads and utility sites for businesses and municipalities.

How long does it last?

Total vegetation control is designed to keep treated areas free of unwanted vegetation for the entire season.
