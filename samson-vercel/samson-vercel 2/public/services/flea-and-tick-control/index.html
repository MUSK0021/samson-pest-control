Flea & Tick Control

Where ticks wait in your yard.

  • Tall grass and overgrown lawn edges
  • The line where your lawn meets the woods
  • Leaf litter, brush piles and ground cover
  • Stone walls and woodpiles
  • Shady spots where pets like to rest

At home

Make your yard less welcoming to ticks.

Treatments work best alongside a few simple habits recommended by the CDC.

Source: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, Preventing Tick Bites

  • Keep grass mowed and clear away leaf litter.
  • Place a 3 foot wide barrier of wood chips or gravel between your lawn and wooded areas.
  • Stack firewood neatly in a dry, sunny spot.
  • Check yourself, your kids and your pets after time outdoors.
  • Ask your veterinarian about tick prevention for your pets.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Still wondering about something? Call 570-278-0325 and talk to a real person.

How many flea and tick treatments are included?

Both the Home Protection Plus Flea and Tick Control plan and the All In One Package include 3 flea and tick treatments.

When are ticks most active?

According to the Pennsylvania Department of Health, blacklegged tick nymphs are most active in late spring and early summer. Adult ticks are active in fall and can be out on winter days above freezing.

Do you treat for mosquitoes too?

Yes. Mosquito control is part of our lawn care services. Ask about it during your free estimate.
