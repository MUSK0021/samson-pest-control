Know what is in the wood before you sign.

A home is the biggest purchase most families ever make. Our real estate termite inspections look for evidence of termites and other wood destroying insects, so buyers, sellers, lenders and agents can move forward with confidence. We are Pennsylvania certified in the Wood Destroying Pest category.

Termite & WDI Inspections

What we look for.

  • Mud tubes on foundation walls, piers and sill plates
  • Swarmers or discarded wings near windows and doors
  • Damaged, blistered or hollow sounding wood
  • Frass, the sawdust like debris left by wood borers
  • Moisture problems that attract wood destroying insects

Services

Wood destroying insects in Pennsylvania.

  • Eastern subterranean termites

    The most common termite in Pennsylvania. Colonies live in the soil and build mud tubes to reach wood.

  • Carpenter ants

    Large black ants that hollow out moist or damaged wood to nest. They do not eat it.

  • Carpenter bees

    Bees that bore round, half inch holes in untreated decks, eaves and fascia.

  • Powderpost beetles

    Tiny beetles whose larvae turn wood to fine powder, leaving pinhole exit holes.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Still wondering about something? Call 570-278-0325 and talk to a real person.

When do I need a termite inspection?

Many lenders and buyers ask for a wood destroying insect inspection during a real estate transaction. Check what your agent or lender requires, then call us to schedule.

Do termites live in Northeast Pennsylvania?

Yes. The eastern subterranean termite is found throughout Pennsylvania. Spring swarms of winged termites are often the first sign homeowners notice.

I saw winged insects inside. Are they termites?

Maybe. Termite swarmers and flying ants look alike. Termites have straight antennae, a thick waist and wings of equal length. Save a sample if you can and give us a call.
