FAQ

Questions, answered

Still wondering about something? Call 570-278-0325 and talk to a real person.

How much does home pest control cost?

Home Protection Plans start at $45 a month, and adding rodent bait station maintenance is $60 a month. Every home is different, so we start with a free estimate and consultation.

What if pests come back between visits?

Call us. If pests return in between scheduled visits, so do we, at no charge to you.

Are treatments safe for my kids and pets?

Safe and effective solutions are our mission. Your technician will explain each treatment and tell you exactly when kids and pets can return to treated areas.

Which pests are covered?

Home plans cover common household pests like ants, spiders, crickets and centipedes. Some exclusions may apply, and we will walk you through exactly what your plan covers during your free estimate.
