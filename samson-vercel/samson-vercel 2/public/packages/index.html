FAQ

Package questions

Still wondering about something? Call 570-278-0325 and talk to a real person.

How much are the packages?

The Home Protection Plan starts at $45 a month and Home Protection Plus Rodent Control is $60 a month. The flea and tick and All In One packages are priced for your property after a free estimate.

What is free emergency service?

Every home protection package includes free emergency service. If pests return in between scheduled visits, so do we, at no charge to you.

What does "some exclusions may apply" mean?

Home plans cover common household pests. A few pests or situations may need a separate service, and we will explain exactly what your plan covers before you sign up.

Can I add lawn care or tick treatments later?

Yes. You can move up to Home Protection Plus Flea and Tick Control or the All In One Package at any time. Just give us a call.
