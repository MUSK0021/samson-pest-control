In our words

Meet Samson, an English Mastiff. A strong, proud, beautiful, powerful dog. Samson was always playful, full of life and dedicated to his family. A protector, a loving soul and a friend.

Samson passed away on September 3, 2017. This strong, powerful dog was conquered by something as small as a pencil point: a blacklegged tick, also known as a deer tick.

We all saw Samson just days before he passed. We remember standing in the living room, full of family and friends, with sadness hanging over the home like dark clouds. Samson lay on that big futon taking labored breaths. The lumps in our throats and the sadness in our hearts were overwhelming.

When we left that evening, we felt there was something we needed to do. Too many pets and loved ones were suffering from Lyme disease, and some were losing the battle. People were getting sick with stiffness and pain, going through round after round of medication, and animals were suffering the same way. Why is it that something so tiny can cause such huge pain and despair?
